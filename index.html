JCR Query & Custom Oak Index Optimization Simulator

Simulate how Saurav's custom Oak Index definitions (Lucene & Property) eliminate repository traversal bottlenecks on AEMaaCS.

Why Custom Oak Indexing Matters in AEMaaCS:

• Unindexed queries scan tens of thousands of JCR nodes, triggering slow DAM search responses and Oak cursor warnings.

• By defining evaluatePathRestrictions="{Boolean}true", includedPaths, and targeted property rules, queries resolve directly from index entries in milliseconds.
